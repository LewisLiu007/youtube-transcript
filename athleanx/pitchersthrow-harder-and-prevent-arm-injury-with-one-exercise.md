[Music] [Music] The follow through jump out is an exercise that we use to develop and train the braking system of the shoulder. Sure, it's important to develop the muscles that actually accelerate the shoulder and throw the ball, but just as important is to develop the muscles that slow down the shoulder and protect it. An exercise like this does just that. What we have to do is anchor tubing low to the ground about a foot or so off the ground. We start in a cocking position.
