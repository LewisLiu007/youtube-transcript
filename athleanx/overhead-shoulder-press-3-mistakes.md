press, your shoulder blades are actually artificially getting involved in the movement. If you're pushing into the bench your shoulder
blades are not allowing the freedom of movement – this upper rotation – to complete an
overhead press. We know that in order to get up over our head

your overhead press. What happens then is, you start to see breakdown
in some of the muscles that are responsible for supporting that overhead press. I'll see rotator cuff issues develop from
